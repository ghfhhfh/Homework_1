History of Umbrella

Beginnings (1960s)

The origins of the Umbrella Corporation lie with the eugenics movements that dominated Europe and North American debate in the early 20th century.

Its three most prominent founding members were:

  • Oswell E. Spencer, Earl Spencer
  • Edward Ashford, 5th Earl Ashford
  • James Marcus
